# Wispry Design Context

## Brand Surface
The landing page should feel like a trustworthy Mac utility with a small craft edge: warm paper, deep green control surfaces, restrained accent green, clear product proof, and no generic SaaS gloss. The product preview should carry the story more than abstract claims.

## Product Surface
The app UI should feel native, compact, and status-driven. Use system typography, predictable AppKit controls, visible permission states, and simple action labels. Brand color is allowed, but task clarity wins over atmosphere.

## Color Roles
- Ink: deep green-black for app and proof panels.
- Paper: warm off-white for marketing sections.
- Accent: yellow-green for active recording, success, and primary highlights.
- Warning: muted red only for errors or risky states.
- Muted text: warm gray-green, never so low contrast that status copy disappears.

## UI Rules
- Buttons must describe the action, not the implementation.
- Recording, permission, paste, and shortcut failures need visible recovery paths.
- Mobile landing content should keep CTAs visible and avoid giant empty viewports.
- Avoid placeholder demos. If media is not ready, frame the section as a product walkthrough instead of pretending video exists.
