Privacy first

Your dictation tool should not feel creepy.

Wispry is built local-first: your cleanup rules, snippets, dictionary terms, and history live on your Mac, and the text polish happens inside the app before anything is pasted.

On your Mac

History and cleanup stay local.

Recent dictations, reusable snippets, dictionary hints, and cleanup preferences are stored in local app settings. Wispry does not need an account to remember them.

Speech recognition
  • Mac permission gateMicrophone and Speech Recognition access are requested by macOS.
  • On-device when availableWispry asks Apple Speech for local recognition when your Mac and language support it.
  • No live transcript wallThe floating bubble stays minimal while you speak.
