# TypeLocal Product Context

## Register
brand + product

## Product Purpose
TypeLocal is a native macOS dictation utility for people who want private speech-to-text in the app they were already using. They click a text field, press a trigger or the floating bubble, speak, and get text pasted back into that same field. It records speech locally, transcribes through Local Whisper with Apple Speech fallback, keeps common self-corrections and spoken punctuation under control, then copies and pastes the cleaned result back into the target app.

## Primary Users
- Mac power users who write many short messages, emails, comments, and notes each day.
- Builders, creators, and community operators who want a lightweight alternative to subscription dictation tools.
- Privacy-sensitive users who need plain language about local recognition, microphone, Apple Speech fallback, local settings, clipboard, and Accessibility permissions.

## Buyer Promise
Click anywhere, speak privately, and get text in place without building a new writing workflow around the tool.

## Product Principles
- Do not silently change meaning. Cleanup must be conservative and test-backed.
- Explain system permissions before macOS asks for them.
- Treat paste reliability as the product, not plumbing.
- Make the floating bubble calm, readable, and easy to escape.
- Use honest privacy copy: TypeLocal has no account, no TypeLocal server, no hosted transcript history, and stores no audio; local transcript history, settings, dictionary terms, and cleanup are local; Local Whisper is the primary private model and Apple Speech is the fallback.

## Tone
Direct, calm, practical, slightly opinionated. Avoid hype, fake enterprise language, and claims that exceed the implementation.
