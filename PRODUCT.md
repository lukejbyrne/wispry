# Wispry Product Context

## Register
brand + product

## Product Purpose
Wispry is a native macOS dictation utility for people who think out loud but need clean text in the app they were already using. It records short bursts of speech, keeps common self-corrections and spoken punctuation under control, then copies and pastes the cleaned result back into the target app.

## Primary Users
- Mac power users who write many short messages, emails, comments, and notes each day.
- Builders, creators, and community operators who want a lightweight alternative to subscription dictation tools.
- Privacy-sensitive users who need plain language about microphone, Apple Speech, local settings, clipboard, and Accessibility permissions.

## Buyer Promise
Speak the messy version, get clean text in place, without building a new writing workflow around the tool.

## Product Principles
- Do not silently change meaning. Cleanup must be conservative and test-backed.
- Explain system permissions before macOS asks for them.
- Treat paste reliability as the product, not plumbing.
- Make the floating bubble calm, readable, and easy to escape.
- Use honest privacy copy: local settings and cleanup are local; speech recognition depends on Apple Speech behavior and on-device availability.

## Tone
Direct, calm, practical, slightly opinionated. Avoid hype, fake enterprise language, and claims that exceed the implementation.
